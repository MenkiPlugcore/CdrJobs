# CdrJobs v0.4.0 — Oath of the Ancient Grove

Adds **Ironbark Warden (Lumberjack)**.

## Unique mechanics
- Natural-log profession XP
- Player-placed logs are persisted and give no XP
- Placed-log identity follows piston movement
- Explosion cleanup prevents stale tracking rows
- Ironbark chopping streak gives brief Resistance

## Path
Timberborn, Whispering Axe, Ironbark, Grove Rhythm, Forest's Favor, Spiritwood, Oath of Yggdrasil.

## Trials
- Trial of Timber: 750 natural logs
- Trial of Ancient Grove: 3000 logs + 300 elder logs

## Testing
- Natural logs reward XP
- Placed logs do not reward XP after restart
- Move a placed log with piston: still no XP
- Explode a placed log: tracking cleans up
- Ironbark trigger works after chopping streak
- Grove Rhythm cooldown persists
- Existing three professions remain intact
